CARETECH • VOICE AI • CONNECTED DEVICE
01

VoiceBridge

One touch to reach your family

VoiceBridge is a one-touch voice connection and safety platform designed for elderly people who struggle with smartphones, including people with dementia and cognitive or speech difficulties

An elderly parent can simply press the VoiceBridge button to tell their family “I want to talk.” Relatives can connect or send their own voice directly into the home

Prototype / Pre-PilotExplore project ↗
Learn more
What VoiceBridge does

VoiceBridge is built around an elderly person who may no longer be comfortable with a smartphone. The home device reduces family contact to one clear physical action: press the button when you want to talk.

Relatives use a mobile interface to receive the request, connect, or send short messages in their own voice directly into the home. A separate wearable SOS channel is being developed for situations when help is needed.

The roadmap adds AI companionship, reminders, selected safety signals and care-provider tools while keeping family communication at the center.

CONVERSATIONAL AI • CONSUMER DEVICES
02

Lunara AI

Giving physical products a voice

Lunara AI brings conversational intelligence into toys and connected consumer products through voice interaction, AI and connected hardware

Prototype / Platform Development
Learn more
What Lunara AI does

Lunara AI is an embedded conversational layer for toys and other physical consumer products. It combines microphones, speakers, connectivity, cloud AI and product-specific dialogue logic.

The goal is to let manufacturers add natural voice interaction without building an entire AI stack from scratch. The platform can support direct voice connection, scenario-based behavior and hybrid local-cloud functions.

The teddy-bear prototype is the first proof of the interaction model, with a broader B2B platform for manufacturers as the longer-term direction.

VERTICAL AI • WINE TECHNOLOGY
03

WINE AI

AI meets Moldova's wine culture

WINE AI explores how conversational AI can create a new digital layer for the wine industry through a multilingual digital sommelier

Development
Learn more
What WINE AI does

WINE AI is a multilingual conversational sommelier designed for wineries, wine tourism, events, retail and visitor centers. A guest can ask naturally about a wine, grape variety, winery, food pairing or where to buy.

The system can work as a mobile experience, web interface or physical kiosk, creating one conversational layer over structured wine and winery knowledge.

The initial focus is Moldova, where the technology can make local wine discovery easier for international visitors and create new digital touchpoints for wineries.

AI SECURITY • FAMILY SAFETY
04

FamilyKey

Trust in the age of synthetic voices

FamilyKey is a family-first verification concept designed to protect people from voice cloning, deepfake and impersonation scams

Prototype
Learn more
What FamilyKey does

FamilyKey gives relatives a simple shared way to verify one another when a suspicious call or voice message appears. The concept focuses on confirmation rather than trying to detect every possible deepfake.

It is designed to remain easy enough for older family members to use and to work without requiring them to understand how AI voice cloning works.

The broader opportunity includes family safety, banks, telecom operators, insurers and organizations serving vulnerable users.

AI Model Selector

The right AI for the right task

AI Model Selector is designed to help businesses and developers choose AI models by balancing task fit, quality, latency and cost

Development
Learn more
What AI Model Selector does

AI Model Selector helps teams compare models against the task they actually need to solve instead of choosing by brand recognition alone.

The selection logic can consider quality, latency, context length, modality, provider availability and cost, then recommend or route to an appropriate model.

The intended value is lower AI operating cost, faster technical decisions and a clearer way to manage a rapidly changing model ecosystem.
